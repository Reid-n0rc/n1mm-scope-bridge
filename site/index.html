Your Yaesu radio's waterfall, in N1MM Logger+

N1MM Scope Bridge reads the spectrum scope built into Yaesu radios and shows it in N1MM Logger+'s Spectrum Display window, the same way Icom radios work today. It starts with the Yaesu FT-710, and more Yaesu models are planned.

How it works

  1. The radio streams its scope. Recent Yaesu radios send their spectrum scope to the PC on a channel of its own, separate from CAT. On the FT-710 it arrives over the same USB cable, next to the usual CAT ports.
  2. The bridge reads it. N1MM Scope Bridge reads that stream on your station PC.
  3. N1MM+ draws it. The bridge sends each spectrum line to N1MM+'s Spectrum Display, which you set to show it.

N1MM+ keeps full CAT control of the radio. The bridge never uses the CAT port and never transmits.
